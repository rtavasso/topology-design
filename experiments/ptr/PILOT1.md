# Rung-0 pilot 1 (27 Oct 2026): 60 runs, 6,000 steps, Kaggle 2×T4

Raw results: `results_data/pilot_*.json.gz` (one per run). Generated tables follow; the reading is in `DESIGN.md` §10
(gate outcomes) and history Entry 55.

**Summary.**
- **G2 passes.** LO (oracle halting, injection on) at lr 1e-3: 1.00 for every k ≤ 24 and 0.89 at k = 32, trained on
  k ≤ 8 only; all eight hops learned by 4,000 steps. lr 3e-3 diverges. **Without injection (LOni) the block never
  learns the second hop at any lr.**
- **LF overthinks.** At its training depth (10 loops) LF is 1.00 for k ≤ 8; at 40 loops its accuracy *rises* with k
  (0.38 at k = 1 → 0.99 at k = 32): the answer state is not a fixed point beyond the training horizon, so extra loops
  erode it.
- **LH-s collapsed** in all 24 configurations: λ → 1 at step 1, perfect at k = 1, chance elsewhere. Cause: the state
  head started at λ ≈ 0.5 (halting mass on steps 1–2, so deep steps got no learning signal and early halting became
  optimal), while the index head started at λ ≈ 0.12 and learned a constant depth of 8 (LHx at lr 3e-4: 1.00 for
  k ≤ 8). PonderNet's rich-get-richer failure, triggered by an init asymmetry of ours. Fixed by `halt_bias_init = −2`
  for every head; pilot 2 re-runs LH-s.
- **Static arms barely learn in 6,000 steps:** S8 (7.3M parameters) 0.24 at k = 2, S2 at chance for k ≥ 2.
  Pilot 2 gives them 15,000 steps at gentler learning rates before anything is concluded.
- **Cost:** ≈ 0.06 s per run-step for a looped arm at T = 10, batch 64 (6-stack: 37 min for 6,000 steps).
