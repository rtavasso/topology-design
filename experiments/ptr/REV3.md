## What was not preregistered, and matters more

1. **Extrapolation is reached and then lost.** At 4,000 steps LH-s and LH-Δ (×1) extrapolate to k = 16 in 8/10 seeds
   each (means 0.89 and 0.88, the level of LO and LR); by 8,000 steps 0/10 and 1/10 remain (0.22, 0.65). LH-s24 keeps
   it through 8k (5/5, 0.96); LO and LF never lose it. Every head's chasing λ is 0.000 from step 2,000 on, so the loss
   is not the head drifting: it is the block, trained jointly, losing its ability to iterate past the trained range
   while staying perfect inside it. Pilot 2's "fails beyond 1.5× the horizon" was the late-training state of this.
2. **LH-Δ with a ×10 head learning rate (gate, 2 seeds): 1.00 at every k ≤ 32, steps k + 1.1 (cheaper than the
   one-parameter rule's k + 1.3), LR on its own block 1.00 everywhere, and no decay (0.85 → 0.91 → 1.00 → 1.00 at
   k = 16 over training).** The heads reached w_KL = −3.3 and −5.1 (×1: −0.66). Exploratory: 2 seeds, and the
   preregistered rule put the 10-seed confirmatory cell at ×1 because both multipliers passed in range — the rule
   was blind to out-of-range accuracy by design, and it is the thing that keeps this a pilot result.
3. **Oracle halting with a dominant one-hop share does not learn the hop.** 0/10 runs at tails ≤ 10⁻¹ and 1/10 at
   factor 1: a block that is never iterated on the dominant examples learns a one-loop map and never the reusable hop.
   The pooling test's clean block arm failed for a reason that is itself a result about pooling: the shared block
   pools signal across depths only if training iterates it on every example. A learned policy that halts short
   chains early would do the same to its block (L23's rich-get-richer, seen from the data side).
4. **Candidate mechanism for 1–2.** The KL-to-prior term is absorbed by whichever part can move. At ×1 the head's
   weights move little (|w| ≈ 0.7–1.9 after 8k steps) and the term reaches the block through the head's input
   (LN(h) or the change features), pushing intermediate states to look "settled"; inside the training range the
   cross-entropy holds the block to the answer, outside it the pressure wins and the block settles short. At ×10 the
   head absorbs the term itself (its KL term is the largest, 2.1 nats; its block is clean). Testable: β = 0, a
   stop-gradient from the head into the block, ×10 at 10 seeds, LH-s at ×10, evaluations every 1k steps.
