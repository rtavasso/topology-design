## 1. Summary

1. **Plasticity after commitment is a property of the signal: exact targets and search move committed routers; the own-policy samplers do not, and they take the committed solution apart.** From the E-committed routers under the local-trigger shift (δ = 0, p_P = 0.3): RT 2/2 (inside 100 steps), EI16 4/5, EX 3/5 (+ 1 R-like), PF 3/5, PF-IS16 1/5, IW16 0/5 at the end (one IW16 run held the moved program for 1,000 steps and then collapsed). The same split at both lower doses and from the L routers. The preregistered reading row that applies: "EX moves, IW16 does not → sampling, not self-consistency, is the lock." *Optimization.*
2. **The gate's coverage numbers did not predict movement in the graded form that was preregistered, and the mechanism behind the E-start EI16 runs is a collective reversal of the beam's merge tie.** PA3's AUROC: within EI16 0.705 (between the falsifier 0.6 and the bar 0.75), pooled over the learned signals without PF / RT 0.821 (holds, carried by EI16 having non-zero coverage where IW16, PF-IS16 and EX have none). The per-seed rows for s3 and s6 (coverage 0.01–0.02 → "do not move") were falsified: both moved. On the E start the run's own coverage goes 0.00 → 1.00 inside one 100-step window and the move follows at once, at a time the gate number does not predict (s6 inside the warm-up, s3 after 1,300 steps, s5 after losing its 0.47 to 0.00 first); on the L start coverage is 0.72–0.84 from step 0 on every router and one of three never moves. *Mechanism (E start); coverage is not the limiting quantity from L.*
3. **PF parks every router, from either start, at one intermediate state within 100 steps and leaves it by a sudden escape, or not within the budget.** From E the HOP − PREV margin at the relabeled class drops from +21 to +32 to ≈ +1.3 (p(PREV) ≈ 0.15) in 100 steps; from L, PF first *removes* the correct PREV (p 1.00 → 0.05–0.15) and parks at the same state. A deterministic committed router hands the sampled fixed prior sixteen identical samples plus the 6–8 % label-reaching decoy routes; the outcomes (the SET at the flag lost in 4/5, 3/3, 3/3 from E and 3/3 from L) are consistent with review 2's decoy-sample mechanism, which was not itself measured. *Mechanism (consistent with).*

## 4. Mechanisms

### 4.1 Search from the E start: the merge tie reverses collectively; from the L start coverage is not the limit

The ε-mixed beam under an E-committed router keeps E's per-episode route (SET at the unflagged predecessor), inexpressible as a rule, or the oracle's L route, and the merge keeps the more probable prefix (code review 1, item 4). The curves record the run's own coverage (`search.oracle_kept_P` on the criterion set) every 100 steps:

From the E start, movement coincides with the tie reversing on the criterion set (0.00 → 0.92–1.00 inside one 100-step window: s3 at 1,300 / 300 / 900 for p_P 0.3 / 0.15 / 0.08, s2 at 2,500 for 0.08, s5 at 500, ε 0.3 s3 at 500); the time of the reversal is not predicted by the gate number (s6 at 0.02 reversed inside the warm-up, s3 at 0.01 took 300–1,300, s5 at 0.47 first lost its coverage, s2 at 0.000 took 2,500 at one dose and did not reverse at two). s2's margin drift from +22 to +6–12 happens with coverage exactly 0.00, so it is not "covered words pulling PREV"; the kept E route visits the predecessor, not the class, and the drift comes through shared parameters or through the lost SET at the flag (p(SET) 0.83 → 0.33–0.54). From the L start coverage is high from the first step on every router and L s1 does not move, so coverage is not the limiting quantity there (§6). Over the 17 EI16 runs: coverage ≥ 0.005 → 10/13 moved (the exceptions L s1 and two dose runs that ended at 0.93–0.94 after reaching 1.00); exactly zero → 1/4, and that group is one router (E s2) under four conditions. ε 0.3 helped only s3 (500 against 1,400), as the §7a reading-table note expected.

Ten of eleven EX-from-E runs lose the SET at the flag within 0–500 steps of the end of warm-up, at every dose (4/5, 3/3, 3/3); the event is sudden (p(SET) 1.00 → 0.00 between two points, SET action accuracy 1.00 → 0.00) and **invisible in the loss** (s3: 0.51–0.52 before, 0.50–0.53 after — under δ = 0 the committed E route is already wrong on every P-word, so losing it changes nothing the loss sees). Where the router moves, it moves *out of that state*, with SET at the flag and PREV at the class appearing in the same 100-step window: 4/5 at p_P 0.3 (s1, s5, s6→R, and s2 which never entered it), 1/3 at 0.15 (s2, twice), 0/3 at 0.08. The controls: 0/5 EX routers continued on the unchanged δ = 1 stream lost anything in 3,000 steps (loss 0.07, p(SET) 1.00, margins intact); phase 1 had one such event in 7 × 2,500 steps. Overall 6/11 end halting against 1/12 without the shift (Fisher p ≈ 0.02); at the standard dose alone (1/5) it is not distinguishable from the spontaneous rate. EX from scratch on δ = 0 never acquires the SET (0/3, the all-H stall of rung 2, p(SET) ≈ 0.22 throughout).

A reading, *not isolated by an intervention*: on a P-word whose committed route is wrong, every route the own policy weights is wrong, the likelihood is flat across them at the readout floor, the own posterior over them is the policy itself, and the step cost λ·E[steps] is the only consistent per-word gradient — it removes the SET (the step that starts the detour) and halts at the flagged pair. Why the escape then happens, and why it is rarer at the low doses while the loss of the SET is not, this reading does not say as written (both the oracle route's exact gradient and the step cost are per-P-word terms, so the dose should cancel); an EX arm with λ = 0 and the posterior on the oracle route logged every 100 steps would test it. For IW16 the end-of-run halting mass is a continuum (0.00 to 0.78 across the 11 E-start runs) rather than EX's bimodal 0 / 0.6–0.86, so its "3/5, 2/3, 1/3" is threshold-sensitive and runs the other way; it is reported, not read.

### 4.4 The sampled fixed prior: the committed router is its worst proposal

Review 2 (Q3) predicted from the gate that under a committed E router 6–8 % of P-word queries have a label-reaching sample at step 0, none of them the oracle route (they reach the label through the decoy chain), and that under PF-IS16's π₀/π weights such a sample is the whole target for that query and teaches HOP at the flag. Observed: PF-IS16 from E ends with the SET at the flag gone (p(SET) 0.00–0.29; first deviation `SET>HOP` on 219–222 of 250 queries) in 4/5 at 0.3 and 3/3 at each dose; from L in 3/3 (PREV at the class lost as well). From scratch the same estimator learns δ = 0 in 3/3, because a young stochastic policy gives it sixteen different samples. On the unchanged δ = 1 stream from a committed L router PF-IS16 lost the program in one of two (0.96 → 0.01 between steps 800 and 900) and degraded the other (in4 0.90), where PF (exact) moved both routers cleanly to E (§7). The weights themselves were not logged, so the decoy mechanism is consistent with the records, not demonstrated.

## 10. What this means (reading against DESIGN §7's table and the ledger)

**Risk 8 (plasticity after commitment).** Commitment is not sticky for the exact targets (RT, EX, PF) or for search from the E start (EI16 4/5), and it is a trap for the own-policy samplers: IW16 and PF-IS16 do not merely fail to move, they take the committed solution apart (halting; the all-H route), PF-IS16 even on unchanged data. DESIGN's own table ("how its targets can see the new PREV at the committed policy") ordered the signals right at the extremes and was wrong in the middle and about the quantity: EX's exact posterior mass on the oracle route (< 0.005) did not stop it, because its moves started from the halting state the shift pushed it into, not from the committed policy the gate measured; EI16's beam coverage mattered as zero against non-zero and reversed collectively; PF's exactness put it on a plateau from either start. The synthesis of Entry 70 (search supplies targets, a fixed prior breaks ties) survives as a statement about plasticity in a narrower form: search is plastic from the E start where its support touched the alternative at all, exact targets reach the alternative by routes the gate did not foresee, and sampled own-policy targets are not plastic.

**What was wrong in the preregistration, and what is post-hoc here.** The coverage thresholds (0.05 / 0.3) treated a knife-edge as a dial; the EX row treated a posterior mass as a coverage, and the design's own falsifier for that reading (an EX move from L s0–s2) fired; PA4's replacement assumed the ceiling moves everything from L; PA′1 assumed EX's posterior on PP completions would fill them in. Three statements in this document are readings formed after the fact and are labelled as such: the λ-driven loss of the SET under EX (§4.3), the decoy-sample mechanism for PF-IS16 (§4.4, consistent with, not measured), and the representation reading of the L start (§6, testable on the checkpoints).

**Three facts the design did not anticipate**, each one seed-count short of a finding and each with a mechanism we do not have: PF's plateau and escape (§4.2); the own posterior losing the SET as its first act under the shift and moving out of the halting state (§4.3); and the dead zone under shift B (RT on L s0; PF-IS16 at f = 0), where a router between two programs that differ in one decision is wrong on everything.

**For the ladder.** Rung 4 (distillation; D110) is unaffected. The signal for later rungs that train a committed router is narrowed to the exact targets and to search; the own-policy samplers are out as trainers of committed routers; PFB16 (stage 1's F42, a soft target over a searched support) is the untested candidate and its plasticity is the first check of whichever later rung needs a scalable signal. Two cheap derive-step questions are left on the saved checkpoints: the L routers' logit separation at the flag states (§6) and PF's plateau (§4.2).

## 11. Limitations

Seeds are 3–5 per cell (AGENTS rule 4; count predictions on near-bimodal outcomes); the per-seed EI16 claim rests on five routers and the L-s1 anomaly on one. The mechanisms of §4.2, §4.3 and §6 are observations with proposed readings, not derivations or interventions. Checks C were not run. Two cells are unreadable by the preregistered RT rule (L s1 under shift A; L s0 under shift B) and are reported, not read. The two 0.949 runs are not counted as movers. The halting mechanism (§4.3) rests on the loss structure and 6/11 against 1/12, not on an intervention (an EX arm with λ = 0 would be the test).
