# Review 2: the open rows and the stage-2 grid (pre-stage-2 re-review)

**Scope.** DESIGN.md revision 2 at `268efac` (§3, §5.3, §7, §7a, §9, §10a), `results_data/gate_stage1.json`, `results_data/stage1_report.md`, `grids/cm_stage2.json`, `make_grids.py`, `gate.py`, `analyze.py`, the stage-1 records and checkpoints under `results/kaggle_route-cm_stage1/`. Probes were run on the checkpoints with `gate.load_router` (CPU, the gate's own 96 episodes, seed 999, 109 P-word queries); the probe script is in the reviewer's scratch directory, not in the repo.

**Verdict: fix first, then launch.** The grid is well formed (115 runs, names unique, every `init_from` exists, cells and seeds as §3/§6 say, phase-2 steps 3,000 with warm-up 100, p_P and deferral per cell, constant schedule). Three things have to change before stage 2 is launched, because they are part of the preregistration or of what the runs record: (B1) the EX / IW16 / PF-IS16 rows from the L start are set from a coverage number that counts the words the L routers already solve under δ = 0; on the words that must move it is ≤ 0.03 for the seeds in the grid, so the rule gives ≤ 1/3, and the replacement for PA4 has the inference backwards; (B2) the dose cells are evaluated on strata generated at the training p_P, which leaves 33 P-words in `in4` at p_P = 0.08 and makes `moved()` fail a genuinely moved router about a quarter of the time, in the direction PA2 predicts; (B3) §7a misreports EX seed 0 (it learned E and then collapsed at step ≈ 2,000), which changes what the EX stability control is for and requires a "collapsed" outcome in the analysis so that a collapse under δ = 0 is not read as lock-in. All three are small changes. The rest is SHOULD-FIX before stage 2 is *read* (analysis code and text) or MINOR.

## Answers to the seven questions

**Q1 (open rows vs the rule).** Checked every cell of the §7a table against `gate_stage1.json` and the seeds the grid uses (E: 1, 2, 3, 5, 6; E_[:3] = 1, 2, 3; L: 0, 1, 2). Every number quoted in the §7a "what each target sees" table is in the JSON to the stated precision, and the per-seed EI16 predictions follow the rule (s1 0.74 and s5 0.45 ≥ 0.3 → move; s2 0.00, s3 0.009, s6 0.009 ≤ 0.05 → not; ε 0.3 on seeds 1, 2, 3: 0.74 / 0.00 / 0.083 → move / not / reported; dose rows on seeds 1–3: 1/3). Two departures: (i) the rule is a cell-count rule, and at the cell level EI16-from-E has mean coverage 0.24, i.e. "reported"; applying it per seed is a legitimate sharpening (PA3 licenses it) but §7a should say that is what it does; (ii) the L-start rows for EX (0.07–0.09 → "reported"), IW16 and PF-IS16 (0.06 → "reported") are set from a coverage that includes the 7 of 109 P-words every L router already solves under δ = 0 (own posterior ≈ 1.00 on each of those). On the 102 words that have to move the numbers are: own posterior 0.024 / 0.0035 / 0.021 (L s0 / s1 / s2), 16-sample coverage 0.000 / 0.000 / 0.000. By the preregistered rule all three rows are **≤ 1/3**, not "reported". See B1.

**Q2 (withdrawing PA4).** Legitimate, with two conditions. PA4 was a count prediction about phase-2 outcomes; it is being withdrawn on the strength of derive-step quantities (coverage, margins, which decision is already in place) that §7 designated as the inputs for setting the open rows, before any phase-2 outcome exists, so there is no outcome-dependence. Conditions: (a) PA4 goes into §12 / the ledger as *withdrawn before test* with its reason (its premise "two decisions, the first with no in-support read" was wrong on the derive step: PREV at set-here is in place on 6/7 L routers, and the L routers' missing decision has a +5 to +9 margin against the E routers' +21 to +32), not silently replaced; (b) the replacement has to be as falsifiable as what it replaces. As written it is not: "PF, RT and EI16 move in a majority" is a count (fine); "the L-start row of EX … may move" is not a prediction; and the clause "if it does there while staying blind from E, 'plasticity is what the target can see' is confirmed within a single signal" is inverted for the seeds in the grid, where EX's own posterior on the words that must move is 0.004–0.024: by the rule an EX-from-L move would *falsify* the coverage reading, not confirm it. The falsifiable form of "the L start is easier" is signal-specific: for EI16, L ≥ 2/3 against E 2/5 (the one signal where the two starts differ in coverage); PF and RT move from both; IW16 and PF-IS16 move from neither; EX is ≤ 1/3 from both unless L s3 (own posterior 0.21 on unsolved words) and s5 (0.054) are added to the EX cell (see B1).

**Q3 (coverage measures).** The four measures are the right ones for the four target types, with one correction (condition on unsolved words, B1) and one addition. EI16: hard EM on the kept route only, so "the kept route is the oracle's" is the quantity; the kept-route program (`kept_set_late`) is the right companion because on the remaining P-words the kept route is E's per-episode route, a target the router cannot express, which pulls against the oracle targets. EX: the exact posterior over routes under its own policy is p(r | y) = π(r) p(y | r) / q(y); `gate.py` computes π(r*)(1 − ε)/q(y) with q from `marginal` (forced halt at T and the key/dead readout mass included, exactly EX's normalizer), p(y | r*) = 1 − ε because the oracle route halts on the label, and `route_logprob` includes the HALT term for routes that halt before T (the oracle's do: ≤ 11 steps at T = 14). Correct; the clamp at 1 is harmless. The λ·E[steps] term of EX's objective is a separate policy-gradient term, not part of the posterior, so leaving it out is right. IW16 and PF-IS16: "16 samples contain the oracle route" is the right predictor for *both*, and "any label-reaching sample" is not: on the E routers 8.3 % of P-words have a label-reaching sample (probe) and none of those is the oracle route; they reach the label through the decoy chain, and under either weighting (IW16's own-posterior weights, PF-IS16's π₀/π weights) such a sample is the whole target for that query and reinforces HOP at the relabeled class. So `hit_P` measures misdirected gradient, which is worth reporting beside coverage (it is already recorded in-run as `search_init.hit_P` for the sampled arms), not plasticity. One subtlety for PF-IS16 that favours the design's choice: when a PREV sample does occur its weight ∝ π₀(r)/π(r) is enormous and dominates the self-normalized target, so coverage is the switch and the response is near-binary. PF and RT: 1 by construction, agreed.

**Q4 (stage-1 facts in §7a).** Verified against the records: EI16 7/7 L, deterministic, criterion 800 [700–1000]; EX 5/7 pass, seed 4 at 0.929 / 0.904 (sampled 0.899); PFB16 δ = 1 5/5 L (1,100 [900–1,400]), δ = 0 3/3 (800), dose 2/3 (3,800), sampled shortest-correct 0.28–0.37 ("0.3–0.4"); PF-IS16 dose reference 2/3 (2,800); RL16-B 0/2 with ESS 0.01, ACMC16-B 0/2, AC16-B 0/2, RL16+H 0/2 at every β with 1.1 / 7.2–7.3 / 13.5 distinct routes. **Misreported: EX seed 0.** It did not "sit in all-H for all 2,500 steps". Its criterion curve (`cm1_EX_p1_s0.json.gz`) has `min_acc_P` 0.89 at step 600, 0.96 at 700, 0.98–1.00 from 800 to 1,900 with program E (`set_late` 0.0), then 0.00 from step 2,000 to the end; the loss goes 0.08 → 0.26 → 0.50 between 1,900 and 2,100 and stays at 0.50; the final evaluation has `first_dev` = {SET>HALT: 121} at in4 and {SET>HALT: 216} at in8 with `q_steps` 2.2–2.4 and none8 at 1.00. It learned E by step ≈ 700–1,000 and collapsed at ≈ 2,000 into halting at flagged pairs. Seed 4's dip at 2,500 (0.93, margin 24 → 15 over the last 300 steps) is the same instability starting. See B3. Minor: the PF-IS16 dose reference's programs are mix / E while PFB16's are L, so the dose comparison is also a program comparison; worth one clause.

**Q5 (the grid).** 115 runs, 115 unique names, all 37 `init_from` names present in `results/kaggle_route-cm_stage1/ckpt/`. Cells: RT_EA30 2 (E s1, s2), PF / EX / EI16 / PFIS16 / IW16 _EA30 5 each (s1, 2, 3, 5, 6), EI16e3_EA30 3 (s1–3); _EA15 and _EA08 5 signals × 3 (s1–3); RT_LA30 2 (L s0, s1), 5 signals _LA30 × 3 (L s0–2); EX_ES 2 (E s1, s2), EI16_LS 2 (L s0, s1); EX / EI16 / PF / PFIS16 _d0 × 3 (seeds 0–2, warm-up 500, from scratch, p_P 0.3); RT_LB100 2, PF / EX / EI16 / PFIS16 _LB100 × 3 and _LB0 × 2. Every continued run: steps 3,000, warm-up 100, schedule constant, lr 1e-3, deferral and p_P per cell, pp_frac 1.0 / 0.0 for the B cells. Phase-2 details checked in `train_route.run_stack`: the optimizer is built fresh (new moments), `per_run_init_` is overridden by `load_router_state_`, the router-config equality assertion will pass (same `BASE`), the frozen bases depend only on `n_sym`, `d`, `emb_seed`, the criterion set and strata are generated under the phase-2 config, evaluations at 2,500 and 3,000. Pairing is good: cells at the same seed share the δ = 0 stream, and the from-scratch references at seeds 0–2 start from the same initial weights as the phase-1 routers of those seeds. Budget: stage 1 measured 0.082–0.113 s per run-step on a T4 across stack sizes 2–7 (≈ 0.107 for stacks of 2, ≈ 0.09 for 5); stage 2 is 345k run-steps in 39 stacks, most of size 2–3, so ≈ 9.5–10 T4-hours, ≈ 5 h wall on two T4s. `make_grids.py`'s 0.085 s/step estimate (8.1 h) is a little low.

**Q6 (confounds).** *The dose.* Relabel events per batch of 64 episodes: ≈ 64 / 34 / 19 P-word queries at p_P 0.3 / 0.15 / 0.08 (measured P-word fractions 16.6 / 9.6 / 5.6 %, matching §2's 16 / 10 / 6). The other side of the stream, all-H words, carries almost no gradient once solved (the phase-1 loss floor 0.07 is the step cost plus the readout), and weight decay at lr·wd = 1e-5 per step shrinks weights by ≈ 3 % over 3,000 steps, under one logit of a 21–32-logit margin; so the dose acts almost entirely through the relabel count and is clean *on the training side*. It is not clean on the evaluation side (B2). *One decision from E.* Yes: on all five E routers the argmax at the oracle-visited (own flag, 0, 0) states is SET on 128–129 of 129 (p(SET) 0.99–1.00) and HOP on 129/129 of the (own flag, 1, 1) states, so exactly one product-state class has to flip, HOP → PREV, and it is the same class at which the δ = 1 E rule HOPs after its SET. *The margin.* For the E routers HOP is the argmax at every relabeled state, so HOP − PREV is the decision margin, and it is tight per seed (±1 logit around 21–32): the right lock-in measure there. It is the wrong measure for L s0 (S1): at 96 of its 129 relabeled states HALT, not PREV, is the argmax (p(PREV) 0.25 in the gate report), which a HOP − PREV margin of −22 cannot show. Reachability is not the obstacle (CPU §9: PF moved a 17-logit margin in 100 steps); RT will say so for 32.

**Q7 (analyze.py).** `moved()` and `move_step()` are right for shift A (final clean-or-mixture evaluation; `min_acc_P` on the criterion set, confirmed by the next point, minus the warm-up; the from-scratch references subtract their 500). Problems: `prog_label` reads the mixture evaluation (`A.final`) rather than the clean one, which is wrong for the shift-B rows whose endpoint is the clean-strata program; the `_LB` rows have no filling-in endpoint (`moved` tests in4 / in8, pp8 is only a mean) and PA′1 fixes no threshold; the per-seed coverage map uses the all-words coverage (B1) and pools PF and RT at coverage 1.0 into the AUROC, which the IW16 / PF-IS16 rows at 0.00 then make trivially high; there is no "collapsed" outcome (B3); the dose rows report time in steps only. Program labels under δ = 0 are fine as they are: `program_stats` marks "late" by the δ = 0 oracle's PREV symbols, a moved router reads L, E is inexpressible, and an "E" label there would itself be a bug signal. The EI16e3 → `beam_eps0.3` mapping and the dose rows' use of the p_P = 0.3 gate numbers are correct (coverage is a property of the router per P-word, not of the stream).

## Issues

### BLOCKING

**B1. The L-start rows for EX, IW16 and PF-IS16 are set from an inflated coverage, and the PA4 replacement draws the wrong inference from it.**
Evidence: `gate.what_it_sees` averages over all P-words (`words = hasP`). Every L router already produces the δ = 0 oracle route on the same 7 of 109 P-words (17 of the 129 oracle-visited (own flag, 0, 0) states already SET; data-determined, identical across seeds), and on those the own posterior is ≈ 1.00. Probe on the 102 unsolved words: own posterior on the oracle route L s0 0.024, s1 0.0035, s2 0.021 (grid seeds), s3 0.206, s4 0.027, s5 0.054, s6 0.0037; 16-sample coverage 0.000 on s0–s2, s4–s6 and 0.010 on s3. The beam numbers barely move (0.58–0.68 unsolved vs 0.61–0.70 all), so the EI16 rows stand. §7a's "sees the oracle route from the L start (0.07–0.26)" and "the one cell where a self-consistent exact target has a visible path … if it does there while staying blind from E, confirmed within a single signal" describe seed 3, which is not in the grid, and invert the rule for the seeds that are.
Fix: (1) `gate.py`: add `*_unsolved` variants of `samples_K16`, `own_posterior_on_oracle` and the three beam numbers, restricted to `~route_ok(committed)` (shift A) / `~kept-shortest-already` (shift B), and print both; (2) §7a: set the L-start rows for EX, IW16 and PF-IS16 to **≤ 1/3** by the rule on the unsolved coverage, and rewrite the two sentences quoted above; (3) replace PA4 with count predictions: from L, RT 2/2, PF ≥ 2/3, EI16 ≥ 2/3, EX ≤ 1/3, IW16 ≤ 1/3, PF-IS16 ≤ 1/3; "the L start is easier" stated for EI16 only (L ≥ 2/3 against E 2/5) with its falsifier (EI16 from L ≤ 1/3, or from E ≥ 3/5); record PA4 as withdrawn-before-test in §12 and the ledger with the reason; (4) optional but cheap (+2 runs, ≈ 12 min T4): add EX_LA30 from L s3 and s5 with per-seed rows (s3 reported at 0.21; s5 reported at 0.05, on the threshold), which is the only way the within-EX coverage contrast the replacement text wants can be run.

**B2. The dose cells are evaluated on strata generated at the training p_P, so the movement endpoint loses most of its P-words exactly where PA2 predicts less movement.**
Evidence: `run_stack` builds `strata` and `crit` from the training `dc`; `strata_episodes` → `forced_word(…, "train", cfg.p_P)`. Generated with the grid's seeds: P-words in the 250-episode forced strata at p_P 0.3 / 0.15 / 0.08 are in4 136 / 77 / 33 and in8 222 / 144 / 107. `moved()` needs ≥ 0.95 at in4 *and* in8: at in4 with 33 words that is ≤ 1 miss, so a router at a true 0.97 reads "not moved" with probability 0.26 at p_P 0.08 and 0.20 at 0.15, against 0.12 at 0.3 (binomial; at a true 0.98: 0.14 / 0.07 / 0.02). The same applies to `move_step` (the criterion set has ≈ 86 P-words at 0.08 against ≈ 255 at 0.3) and to the margin curve, which has a third as many relabeled states. PA2's "PF still moves in ≥ 2/3 at 0.08" and "time to move grows as p_P falls" are then confounded with evaluation sample size, in the predicted direction. The clean-evaluation slot only triggers on `pp_frac > 0`.
Fix: in `run_stack`, make the clean evaluation the *standard* evaluation: `dc_clean = RouteConfig(**{**dc.dict(), "pp_frac": 0.0, "p_P": 0.3})` whenever `tc["clean_eval"]` and (`dc.pp_frac > 0` or `dc.p_P != 0.3`); generate the criterion set at p_P 0.3 for the dose cells too (keep the training config for shift B, whose criterion set carries `min_acc_pp`). `moved()` and `fin` already prefer `clean`. Add a test that the clean strata of a p_P = 0.08 config have the p_P = 0.3 P-word counts. This also makes the margin curves of the dose series comparable. (If the eval is decoupled, a p_P = 0 anchor cell, 2 runs of PF or EX from E, would give the dose series its true zero: margin drift with no relabel data; optional.)

**B3. §7a misstates EX seed 0; the correct fact changes the stability control's role and needs a "collapsed" outcome in the analysis.**
Evidence: Q4 above (learned E by ≈ 700–1,000, collapsed at ≈ 2,000 into halting at flagged pairs; seed 4 dipping at 2,500 with its margin falling 24 → 15). EX at the constant lr can leave its committed solution spontaneously within the phase-2 budget, which (i) makes EX_ES a load-bearing null (the rate of spontaneous collapse under EX's own signal is what "EX from E did not move" is read against), and (ii) means a phase-2 EX run can end with `min_acc_P` 0 for a reason that is neither lock-in nor blindness. `moved()` returns False for both; the HOP − PREV margin is blind to HALT winning (it returned to +21 after s0's collapse while the router halted at every flag).
Fix: (1) correct §7a and the stage-1 paragraph; (2) `analyze.py`: add an outcome `collapsed` (final in8 `first_dev` mass on `*>HALT` ≥ 0.5, or in8 `min_acc_P` < 0.5 with the run's own-signal loss back at its all-H / halt level) beside `moved` and `locked` (first deviation `PREV>HOP` at the relabeled class), and print the final `first_dev` histogram per phase-2 run; (3) raise EX_ES to all five E routers (+3 runs; the base rate so far is 1 collapse in 7 × 2,500 steps) and consider EI16_LS the same way if budget allows; (4) in the reading table, "EX from E does not move" is read only on runs that did not collapse.

### SHOULD-FIX (before stage 2 is read; text before launch)

**S1. L s0 is in the grid and is the one L router where PREV is not in place.** `p_prev_at_class` 0.254, margin HOP − PREV max +13.8; probe: at the 129 oracle-visited (own flag, 1, 1) states its argmax is HALT on 96 and PREV on 33. §7a's "PREV already preferred; p(PREV) 1.00 on six of seven" should say the seventh is seed 0 and is in every L-start cell, so for that router the L start is two decisions (SET at the flag, HALT → PREV at set-here). Fix: say so in §7a; record on the curve the decision margin log π(PREV) − max_{a ≠ PREV} log π(a) (or p(PREV)) beside `margin_hop_prev`, since a two-way margin cannot show a third action winning; the same for the SET margin.

**S2. `analyze.py` changes.** (a) `prog_label` for the phase-2 table should read the clean evaluation when present (as `moved` does); (b) add `filled(r)` for the `_LB` rows: pp8 shortest-correct ≥ a threshold fixed now in PA′1 (the design's own one-lookup policy reaches 0.90 on PP words, so 0.90 is defensible; say which) and the route-type split by mapping the `pp_set_placement` keys `2:a-b-c-d-e` to E-E (1-1-0-0-0), E-L (1-0-1-0-0), L-L (0-1-1-0-0), L-double (0-2-0-0-0), E-double (2-0-0-0-0); (c) the PA3 AUROC: use the unsolved-conditional coverage (B1), report it within EI16 (the 20 EI16 / EI16e3 runs across both starts and the doses, the form §7a calls sharpest) and pooled over the open and predicted rows *without* PF and RT (their coverage is 1 by construction and only inflates it); also print each run's own in-run coverage at step 0 (`search_init.oracle_kept_P` for EI16, `oracle_any_P` and `hit_P` for the sampled arms: the criterion set's 256 episodes under the run's own data, ≈ 2.5× the gate's sample) beside the gate number as a consistency check; (d) the `collapsed` outcome and `first_dev` (B3); (e) for the dose rows, time to move also in relabel events (steps × batch × 6 × P-word fraction), which separates "fewer relabels" from "more background" if the two doses differ in steps but not in events.

**S3. §7a's shift-B row and PA′1 disagree, and one column has no cell.** The row lists IW16 "reported (0.08–0.10)" but there is no IW16 shift-B cell in §3, §6 or the grid; PA′1 predicts EX ≥ 2/3 on PP words while the row says "reported". The `own_posterior_on_oracle` under shift B is the mass on the oracle's E-type PP route (5e-12 to 1e-8 on the L routers), not on "any shortest completion", so it is not the relevant quantity for filling in. Fix: drop the IW16 column from the B row (or add the cell), and keep PA′1's ≥ 2/3 for EX unless the right quantity (the router's posterior mass on the set of shortest label-reaching routes) is computed.

**S4. The continued runs replay the phase-1 pool and batch order.** `EpisodePool(dc, …, seed = s·7919 + 11)` and its sampler `Generator().manual_seed(seed + 7)` are keyed by the run seed and the data config, so EX_ES_s1 draws the identical 60k-episode pool and the identical first 3,000 batch indices that `cm1_EX_p1_s1` trained on; the shift cells get a different pool only because δ or p_P changed. For the stability control that is the strictest possible "unchanged stream", but it cannot distinguish stability from having fit those batches. Fix (cheap): offset the pool seed for runs with `init_from` (e.g. `+ 1000`), or state in §3 that the control replays the training stream. Not a confound for shift A.

**S5. State the coverage sample size next to the rule.** The gate's 109 P-word queries give a binomial SE of 0.03–0.05 at the values that matter; E s3's 0.083 at ε 0.3 and L s5's 0.054 own posterior sit on the 0.05 threshold. Say in §7a that values within one SE of a threshold are "reported", and consider `--n 256` for a rerun of `gate.py` (CPU minutes) so the per-seed rows rest on ≈ 290 words.
